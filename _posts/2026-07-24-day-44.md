what_i_learned: |
           Today reminded me that knowing the research and presenting the research are two                     different skills. I understand the work we have completed, but I am still learning how              to trust what I know and speak naturally instead of depending so heavily on the words on            the screen. The more we practice as a team, the more comfortable I become explaining our            research in my own words.
blockers:   | 
          My biggest personal blocker continues to be reading directly from the screen during                 presentations. I know the material, but once I have a script in front of me, I                      automatically want to read it word for word. Another blocker is that our research paper             is still not complete, so we are continuing to make final adjustments while also                    preparing to present our work.
reflection: |
         Overall, today was a good day. Our professor took us to lunch, which gave us an                    opportunity to step away from the research for a little while and simply enjoy time                  together as a team. After spending so many weeks working, coding, troubleshooting,                   writing, and revising together, the team bonding was definitely appreciated.
