what_i_learned: |
Today is my birthday, but it is also the day we are celebrating the end of our nine-week SAIRI journey. It feels fitting to close one chapter while I am literally beginning another year of my life. Today is filled with joy, love, gratitude, and honestly, a little sadness because an experience that became such a major part of my summer is officially coming to an end.
Over these nine weeks, I learned so much more than I expected. I came into this experience as a Family and Consumer Sciences major and found myself learning alongside mathematicians, working with Python, machine learning models, optimization, public-health data, literature reviews, research papers, and presentations. I learned that I do not have to change who I am or what I study to have a place in AI and research. I can bring the human side of things into these spaces, and that perspective has value.
I also learned that AI can become something much bigger in my future than simply using existing tools. I now know that I can continue learning and eventually create programs of my own, including technology that can make communication more fluid and accessible between teachers and students. I can also see how AI can support my businesses, my work in education, and my desire to serve communities
blockers:   | 
         No blockers today! Today was about presenting the work, supporting my team, and enjoying the final day of the symposium.
reflection: |
When I reflect on the entire experience, it definitely was not always easy. There were coding errors, deadlines, corrections, long days, uncomfortable moments, presentations, last-minute research paper revisions, and plenty of times when I had to learn something completely outside of my comfort zone. But there was also laughter, team bonding, mentorship, new relationships, accomplishments, and moments that reminded me why I continue to say yes to opportunities that challenge me.
I am especially grateful for my team, Dr. Iyiola, and our Graduate Mentor Amara. Somewhere between the research, troubleshooting, presentations, lunches, and long days together, this became more than a summer research program. I gained relationships, memories, knowledge, and experiences that I will carry with me long after SAIRI.
Nine weeks ago, I was learning what was possible with AI. Today, I am thinking about what I can create with it, who I can serve with it, and how I can use everything I learned moving forward. It is bittersweet to see this experience end, but I am proud of how much I learned, how much I grew, and most importantly, that I finished what I started.
Today, I celebrate another year of life and the completion of an unforgettable nine-week journey. This chapter is officially complete, and I am ready to see what I build with everything it taught me.
